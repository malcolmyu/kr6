## Projectile, trap and stun

The original25/30-second projectile uses native bomb motion and next-tick insertion, then queues the trap for the next insertion phase. Primary damage is zero. Land has no hit FX and therefore does not play its inherited tar-impact sound; water uses the original splash and sound. Source sale before landing still leaves an autonomous trap, without registering it on an absent/rebuilt source.

`CatapultTraps` registers with a source that exists when its script starts, plays the native opening sound and runs the18-frame set animation. The ready state has no time limit and checks targets every tick, as the original does; the inherited aura cycle_time does not introduce a polling delay. A target within radius40 starts activate. Twelve ticks later the trigger sound plays and targets are queried again, so leaving or dying during activation can avoid damage. Physical damage is20–30/32–48/48–72, with separate armour application and no inherited tower damage factor. The28-frame activation finishes, hides the trap, removes it from the owner's active count and queues entity removal for the next tick.

Stun insertion is queued after damage and uses1/2/3 seconds. Aura visibility and stun visibility are distinct: a boss can trigger/take damage while rejecting stun, and a killed target rejects next-tick stun insertion. Same rank refreshes the old clock; stronger replaces weaker while preserving the sprite clock; weaker is rejected. Separate stun sources retain separate reference-count contributions. Ignore-stun units retain the native modifier lifetime without becoming stunned. Removal occurs on the queued boundary. Actual GameSession movement stops and resumes through its existing is_stunned guard.

The renderer uses original projectile/set/ready/activate frames, original small/big stun loops, native animation clocks and source loader40FPS. Original purchase/fire/open/trigger audio requests are connected. Global draw sorting, complete native particle/blood/pop effects and the full audio mix remain outside this module's proof.
