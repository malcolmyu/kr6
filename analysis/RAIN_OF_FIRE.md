Current update: the ordinary map power room now supports purchases, branch replacement, per-power reset, explicit equipment slots and saved reloads. Royal reset is connected to the currently implemented Wizard B/Archer B skill controllers. See POWER_ROOM.md; mentions of pending room work below describe the earlier milestone. Full combat/video acceptance remains incomplete.

The overall goal remains incomplete. This implements the fire power's upgrade branches in the existing web engine; it does not prove complete six-flow/native-battle/video/audio parity. The power-room purchase UI is still pending.

Native evidence and implementation:

- `scripts_game.lua31400–31512` is the real rain controller. It waits.33s relative to each burst, scatters subsequent primary meteors, clamps to the closest valid rally lane and optionally emits a cataclysm meteor. Cataclysm picks an active path, one of its three lanes, and a random valid target node, checks a10-node margin and land terrain, and retries at most five times. Failed attempts genuinely produce no extra meteor. The native loop still runs max(meteor_count,cataclysm_count) iterations when cataclysm is disabled.
- `RainControl.ts` matches36 original controller executions across all three maps, three controlled RNG values, base/upgraded configurations and two birth times. This includes actual original path/grid lookups, destinations, burst clocks, failed samples and removal time. Each cast captures its original visible top; queued bullets begin on the following update. This replaces the old absolute burst scheduler and approximate lane clamp.
- Native projectile31517–31655 chooses water/land branches. Water only spawns the original splash; land spawns its crack/scorch, damage aura, optional slow aura, impact FX and optional explosion payload. All34 upgrade/water combinations match original queue records. New templates export the aura and modifier data so session-local upgrade patches affect their actual durations/damage.
- Native aura_apply_mod3915/3938, modifier lifecycle1364, DPS4807/4837, slow5224/5240 and tween systems are executed through original klove.simulation with health enabled. Twenty-four450-tick cases produce10,800 frames covering two birth ticks, base/extended ground duration, overlapping fields, movement out/return during fade, visibility immunity and death. The web module matches queued casts, health, speed, modifier clocks, DPS clock, pending removals, burn alpha/reverse and scorch visibility/alpha/clocks every frame. Aura coroutines must remain live or have actually queued removal; caught native errors cannot silently shorten the oracle.
- Burn modifiers refresh their duration without stacking or resetting the DPS clock. They deal immediate first damage on insertion, then advance the original.25s clock. Native floating damage ranges after+1.5 use LuaJIT integer-bound behavior; the web roll preserves those bounds. A fading burn can receive a timestamp refresh without restarting its dead damage coroutine; re-entry behavior is covered. Slow is a separate×.4 speed contribution and restores by division; its original insert does not reuse the burn's visibility rejection. No blanket immunity rule was invented.
- Native delayed payload31667–31705 waits14ticks, damages eligible targets in radius60 with floor(36−12×elliptical_distance_factor), then completes its37-frame nonloop animation. Two native birth-time cases include horizontal/vertical boundaries, air, boss and immunity. Targets have valid native path nodes so the oracle really hits; zero-hit output is rejected. The target-node guard is bound to the session's actual combat predicate.
- `RainAreas.ts` preserves next-tick modifier insertion, strict native float comparisons, burn fade.75s, independently extended ground/slow durations and queued cleanup. `RainRenderer.ts` draws original burn attachment,1.2-scale looping scorched ground, delayed extra explosion, original anchors/frame ranges/alpha and cleanup. Scorch retains the projectile's original sprite clock across next-tick insertion. Existing meteor flight/shadow rendering remains; full native particle trails have not been established.
